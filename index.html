This domain is used for research

The domain name rktv.top is registered by TorrentFreak, a news site covering copyright, piracy and site blocking.

The name previously appeared on a French list of domains that internet intermediaries are required to block. The registration had lapsed when we took it over. We use it to see how blocking lists handle domains that change hands.

This site does not offer, link to or promote any streaming service.
